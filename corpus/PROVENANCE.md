# Fixture provenance

The current corpus was independently authored on 9 October 2026 by Codex at the
maintainer's request. It is covered by the project's MIT license. All 24 inputs
are constructed by [generate.py](generate.py) from literal invented scenarios;
the generator reads no statement samples, old fixtures or parser outputs.
Its source hash and every generated input/expectation hash are recorded in
[provenance.json](provenance.json).

The main scenario uses 14–16 September 2026, a GBP 500.00 opening balance,
an 18.75 debit and an 80.00 credit: closing balance 561.25. Other scenarios
deliberately omit fields, contradict balances/currencies, reverse movements,
duplicate scopes or supply ambiguous/malformed CSV rows. Names and references
are invented test labels. Account identifiers are constructed with a TEST bank
code, zero-filled BBAN and mod-97 check digits; they are not bank exports or
payment instructions. No account-ownership or routing lookup was performed.

Expected JSON is specified by the generator's independent canonical builders.
They do not call BankParser or its backends. The validation contract labels the
declared closing balance expected and opening plus movements actual.
examples/output is a separate demonstration of actual parser serialization,
regenerated from these new inputs; it is not the correctness oracle.

## Format guidance

Only field semantics and syntax were consulted; no published sample statement
was copied or transformed:

- MT940: [mt-940 tag documentation](https://mt940.readthedocs.io/en/stable/mt940.tags.html),
  account/statement identifiers, opening/closing balances and movements.
- CAMT: [ISO 20022 Payments Maintenance 2009 message definition report](https://www.iso20022.org/message/mdr/12666/download/111),
  BankToCustomerStatementV02 and entry, balance, date and reference semantics.
- OFX/QFX: [FDX OFX specifications](https://financialdataexchange.org/about-fdx/ofx-work-group/)
  and [OFX Banking 2.3 field definitions](https://financialdataexchange.org/common/Uploaded%20files/OFX%20files/OFX%20Banking%20Specification%20v2.3.pdf),
  bank/card accounts, posted transactions, identities and ledger balances.
- CSV: project-defined headers and mapping conventions in the
  [compatibility guide](../docs/compatibility.md).

These are bounded parser scenarios, not certified exports or full XSD/OFX
conformance samples. CAMT intentionally exercises the accepted wrapped Sts/Cd
shape within the existing V02 namespace profile. Broader version-specific schema
validation and real exporter coverage remain roadmap work.

## Reproduction

Run `python corpus/generate.py --check` to verify generated bytes and hashes;
omit `--check` to regenerate. Keep expectations independent of parser output.
Any new input needs an authorship/source and redistribution record when added.
